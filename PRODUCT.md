# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mostly 14 to 30 year olds who found Ogi through a TikTok or Instagram reel and tapped the bio link. They arrive on a phone, thumb-first, often late at night in the same scroll session as the video. They are curious ("what's his streak right now?"), competitive ("could I beat him?"), or both. A second, smaller group is brands and collaborators checking reach before sending an email.

Jobs to be done:

- Fans and challengers: see the current winstreak and the prize for breaking it, watch the best videos, follow the accounts.
- Brands: understand the reach in seconds and find the collaboration email.

## Product Purpose

Ogi's main home on the internet. Ogi plays 1v1 street football against anyone, anywhere, and talks trash while doing it. One rule: first to 3 goals wins.

Six months in on TikTok: every video past 500K views, five past 1M, 21K followers.

The site is the only place the live **winstreak** is published: how many players in a row Ogi has beaten without a loss. Every 5 wins in a row adds €5 to the prize for whoever breaks it (streak 15 means €15). Keeping the streak exclusive to the site is deliberate: it turns curiosity in the comments into visits.

Success: viewers from social land, see the streak within the first screen, and leave either following, sharing the streak, or wanting to challenge him. Brands find the collab email without searching.

## Brand Personality

Cheeky, friendly, competitive. Ogi is a nice guy who still talks trash: playful jabs, never mean. Confidence with a grin. He is himself, not a copy of another creator's persona.

Voice: short, punchy, conversational, a little cocky. Talks directly to the visitor ("Think you can stop it?"). Never corporate, never hostile.

Emotional goals: curiosity (the streak), a "yo, this guy" grin, the itch to challenge him.

## Anti-references

- Generic creator link-in-bio pages (Linktree style stacks of buttons).
- Aggressive, hostile fight-promo tone or anything that reads as bullying.
- Copying another creator's look or persona.
- Corporate sports-brand polish that loses the street feel.
- Neon-on-black "gamer" aesthetics and SaaS landing page templates (hero metric, icon card grids).

## Design Principles

1. **The streak is the headline.** It is the reason to visit; it should be impossible to miss and easy to screenshot.
2. **Street, not stadium.** Rooted in cages, courts, chalk and cones: real places where 1v1s happen.
3. **Grin, not glare.** Trash talk with a smile; copy and motion stay playful.
4. **Send them back to the feed.** Videos live on TikTok and Instagram; the site teases and links out instead of hosting.
5. **Phone first.** Designed for a thumb arriving from a bio link, then scaled up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus, keyboard reachable video choices and dialog. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all entrance and marquee motion. Never use color alone to carry meaning (streak and prize also stated in text).
